Hi, I'm Lili

        
A Salvadoran creator living in Austin, Texas, sharing beauty, lifestyle, food and travel in English and Spanish.

        
UGC Creator & Paid Ad SpecialistUGC & paid ads for beauty & family brands

        
I grew up in San Salvador and went to an English-immersion school from a young age, which is where my love for U.S. culture began. Today I call Austin home.

        
I've always loved taking photos of the little things: the details, moments and memories that make everyday life feel special. I'm also a girly girl at heart. I love makeup, getting dressed up, and watching beauty tutorials to try something new. And I love traveling, exploring new places, and finding delicious spots to eat along the way.

        
I bring all of that into the content I create, in both English and Spanish, sharing beauty, lifestyle, food and the places I love with an audience across the U.S. and Latin America. My goal is to create content that feels personal, pretty, and true to real life.

    Privacy Policy
    
    
    Last updated November 28, 2026
    
    
    This policy explains what information lilimagana.com collects and how it is used. The website was developed and is run by Brendan Fusting in Austin, Texas. Questions about this policy can be sent to bhfusting@gmail.com.
    
    
    What we collect
    
    
    The only personal information we collect is what you choose to type into the collaboration inquiry form: your name, brand or venue, email address, type of project, date or timeframe, and your message. You don't need an account, and browsing the site doesn't require giving us anything.
    
    
    How it's used
    
    
    Your inquiry is used only to reply to you about a possible collaboration. We never sell, rent or share it for marketing, and we don't add you to a mailing list.
    
    
    How your inquiry is delivered
    
    
    When you press "Send inquiry", the form is delivered to Lili's email inbox through Web3Forms, a form-to-email service. After that, it is kept in Lili's email account for as long as needed to follow up on the inquiry.
    
    
    Cookies and tracking
    
    
    This site does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. It remembers your language choice in your own browser so the page opens in the same language next time; that setting never leaves your device.
    
    
    Other services the site uses
    
    
      
      
    • The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log technical details such as your IP address for security.
      • 
      
    • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the fonts load.
      • 
      
    • Some reels play through Instagram's own embedded player. When you open one, Instagram (Meta) loads content and may set its own cookies under Instagram's privacy policy.
      • 
      
    • Links to Instagram and other sites are covered by those sites' own policies.
      • 
    
    
    
    Your choices
    
    
    You can ask what we have from your inquiry, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing bhfusting@gmail.com.
    
    
    Children
    
    
    This site is meant for brands and businesses and is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.
    
    
    Changes
    
    
    If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
